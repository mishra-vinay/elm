Uses the same engine as the live dry runs. Sessions that are far from complete (half days, missing data) are skipped automatically, and the result says how many. Each candle is replayed as a short open-low-high-close path, so 1-minute data is much more faithful than 15- or 60-minute data. Backtests judge an idea, they do not prove future profit: look at the number of trades, the t-statistic and whether both halves of the period agree.
