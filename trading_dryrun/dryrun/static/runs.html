Pick an instrument and its previous-day High/Low appear here.
Several runs at once: start as many as you like (up to 20), on different instruments or on the same instrument with different settings and names to compare them side by side. They all read the same live prices and are independent.
Previous-day High/Low come from the database (daily or stored intraday candles) and then from Dhan's historical API. The rule is the one backtested on silver and Bajaj Finance, which showed no edge there; this is for collecting your own evidence. Defaults are the tested values, not recommendations. Real-order live trading is not enabled in this phase: nothing here ever sends an order.
