Depth recorder records every update of the 5 bid + 5 offer levels, all session, every weekday

InstrumentIdRecording
The recorder starts by itself Monday to Friday at 08:55 IST and stops at 23:59 (MCX closes at 23:30, or 23:55 when US daylight saving has ended). It uses the Dhan token from the Settings page. Data is kept in data/depth.db on the server for 60 days. The exchange feed shows only 5 levels, and orders can be placed and cancelled without trading, so a quantity jump is a hint, not proof of intent.
