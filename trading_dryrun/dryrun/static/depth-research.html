Test order-book and volume ideas on the recorded depth data

Each idea is turned into a fixed rule, "traded" against the recorded best bid and offer (a long buys at the offer and sells at the bid, so the spread is paid) plus a round-trip fee, and judged on the first half of the recorded days against the second half. 39 variants are tested, so a few will look good by chance: a rule is flagged only if it is positive in both halves, has 20+ trades in each, |t| ≥ 3, and wins on at least 60% of its days. With one recorded day nothing can be flagged. This is research on recorded data, not advice, and no orders are ever sent.
