Stored ticks (raw price updates)

Every distinct price update is saved with its arrival time (to the microsecond), roughly one to a few rows per second per instrument. Old ticks are deleted automatically so the database stays small. Download a day first if you want to keep it; candles, runs and trades are never touched.
